# Hotel_Cancellation_Dashboard

📊 Key Components
Overall Metrics:

Total bookings vs. cancellations to measure cancellation rate.

Guest Type Analysis:

Couples, families, and singles compared to see which group cancels most often.

Monthly Trends:

Seasonal peaks in cancellations (e.g., May and August) showing demand fluctuations.

Hotel Type Comparison:

City hotels vs. resort hotels, both in total bookings and cancellations.

Room Status Impact:

Desired vs. undesired room allocations and their effect on cancellations.

🔍 Purpose
To help hotel management reduce cancellations by spotting patterns.

To guide strategic decisions like pricing, promotions, and room allocation.

To improve customer satisfaction by understanding guest behavior.

In short, the project was a data-driven exploration of hotel booking behavior, aiming to minimize cancellations and optimize operations.
